## UI kit, renderer and native foundation

The UI kit, renderer, audio mixer and native app tooling come from
[ps5-homebrew-ui](https://github.com/blackbearreloaded/ps5-homebrew-ui) and the
[PS5 Native App Boilerplate](https://github.com/blackbearreloaded/ps5-native-app-boilerplate),
Copyright (C) 2026 BlackBearReloaded, GPL-3.0-or-later. Files taken from them
keep their copyright lines and SPDX identifiers.

- The shape shader in `src/gfx/gl_batch.cpp` adapts the 2D signed-distance
  functions published by Inigo Quilez
  (https://iquilezles.org/articles/distfunctions2d/), MIT.
- The ELF converter and FSELF writer in `tooling/native/` are derived from
  [SharpProspero](https://github.com/SvenGDK/SharpProspero), Copyright (C) 2026
  SvenGDK, GPL-3.0, translated to C++ and modified by BlackBearReloaded.
- `tooling/native/libc_builder.cpp` and `tooling/native/runtime/` are
  independently authored (GPL-3.0-or-later). The generated `runtime/libc.prx`
  contains project-authored compatibility stubs and loader metadata only; it
  contains no Sony runtime implementation.
